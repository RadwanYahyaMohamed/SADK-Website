Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Who can join Deutsch Klub?

STEM Asyut Deutsch Klub is open to all students at STEM Schools who are interested in learning German — regardless of their current level. Whether you are an absolute beginner or already know some basics, you are warmly welcome. Our peer-mentoring environment means every learner finds support at exactly their level.

When are the club sessions held?

Sessions are held weekly throughout the school year, typically on Sundays from 3 PM to 7 PM. In addition to offline sessions, we provide structured study materials, online quizzes, and final revision sessions before exams. Follow our Facebook page or WhatsApp channel for the latest schedule.

Do I need prior German knowledge?

No prior knowledge is required at all! Our materials cover everything from scratch — starting with the STEM G10 curriculum (Netzwerk Neu, Grammatik Aktiv, A Grammatik, deutsch.com) and extending to A1, A2, and B1 levels. All materials are free, peer-reviewed, and verified by our school's German teacher.

How can I access learning materials?

All learning materials are available for free on this website under the "Learn German" section — organized by grade (G10/G11) and semester. You can also find test banks, interactive quizzes, and a curated resources page. Create a free account to track your progress on the Daily Challenge and unlock all features.
